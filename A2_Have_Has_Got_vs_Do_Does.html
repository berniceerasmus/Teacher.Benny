1. Have/Has Got — Possession

We use have got and has got to talk about possession — things we own, have, or things that exist for us right now.

Türkçe: "Have/has got" sahiplik bildirmek için kullanılır — bir şeye sahip olduğumuzu söylemek için. "Var / yok" anlamına gelir.

Form:

I / You / We / They → have got

He / She / It → has got

Sentence typeExample
PositiveI have got bread. / She has got milk.
NegativeI haven't got cheese. / He hasn't got eggs.
QuestionHave you got apples? / Has she got milk?
Short answerYes, I have. / No, she hasn't.
Örnek çeviri: "Ekmeğim var." = I have got bread.  |  "Peynirim yok." = I haven't got cheese.  |  "Yumurtan var mı?" = Have you got eggs?

Contractions: In spoken and written English, "have got" is almost always contracted. Learn to recognise both the full and short forms:

Full formContraction
I have gotI've got
You/We/They have gotYou've/We've/They've got
He/She/It has gotHe's/She's/It's got
I have not gotI haven't got
He has not gotHe hasn't got
Dikkat: "He's got milk" cümlesindeki "'s" bazen "is" bazen "has" anlamına gelebilir. Burada "has got" anlamındadır (sahiplik), "is" değil.

What "have got" is used for: possessions (I've got a car), relationships and people (She's got two brothers), illness or physical feelings (I've got a headache), and features/characteristics (This flat has got a big kitchen).

Important limit: "have got" is only for possession-type meanings above. It is never used for actions or routines. We cannot say "I've got breakfast every day" — this must be "I have breakfast every day" (an action, so it needs do/does — see below).

2. Have vs Have Got — What's the Difference?

This is where many A2 students get confused, so let's slow down. "Have" and "have got" can both express the same meaning of possession — but they behave grammatically differently.

Türkçe: "Have" ve "have got" genellikle aynı anlama gelir (sahiplik bildirir), ama farklı şekilde davranırlar. Bu bölümde ikisi arasındaki farkı öğreneceğiz.

"Have" acts like a normal, ordinary verb (like "like", "want", "buy"). Because it's an ordinary verb, questions and negatives need the helper verb do/does.

"Have got" is a fixed, two-word structure. Here, "have/has" already acts like a helper verb by itself, so do/does is never added.

Meaning"Have" (ordinary verb)"Have got" (fixed structure)
PositiveI have a car.I've got a car.
NegativeI don't have a car.I haven't got a car.
QuestionDo you have a car?Have you got a car?
He/She/It negativeShe doesn't have a car.She hasn't got a car.
He/She/It questionDoes she have a car?Has she got a car?
Türkçe özet: "Have" sıradan bir fiildir; soru ve olumsuz cümlede "do/does" ister. "Have got" ise kendi içinde yardımcı fiil görevi görür, ayrıca "do/does" eklenmez. İkisi de "sahip olmak" anlamına gelir, ama cümle yapıları farklıdır.

Never mix the two patterns together. This is the single biggest mistake at this level:

✗ Does she have got a car? (mixes both patterns — incorrect)

✓ Does she have a car?    OR    ✓ Has she got a car? (choose ONE pattern, not both)

Which one should you use? Both are correct and both are used every day. "Have got" is extremely common in British spoken English and sounds natural in conversation. "Have" (without "got") is used more in American English, in formal writing, and when the sentence is already using do/does for another reason. As a learner, it's enough to recognise both and be able to use either one consistently — just don't mix them in the same sentence.

3. Do/Does — Actions, Habits & Likes

We use do and does as helper (auxiliary) verbs to talk about actions, habits, and likes/dislikes — things we do regularly, or how we feel about something.

Türkçe: "Do/does" alışkanlıkları, günlük eylemleri ve sevdiğimiz/sevmediğimiz şeyleri anlatmak için kullanılan yardımcı fiildir. Kendi başına "yapmak" anlamına gelir, ama burada soru ve olumsuz cümle kurmaya yardım eder.

Form:

I / You / We / They → do

He / She / It → does

Sentence typeExample
PositiveI like apples. / He buys bread. (no "do" in positive statements)
NegativeI don't like cheese. / She doesn't buy meat.
QuestionDo you like apples? / Does he buy bread?
Short answerYes, I do. / No, he doesn't.
Örnek çeviri: "Elmaları severim." = I like apples.  |  "Peyniri sevmem." = I don't like cheese.  |  "Elmaları sever misin?" = Do you like apples?

Important: We do not say "Do you got...?" — this mixes the two structures. "Got" always goes with "have/has", never with "do/does".

✗ Do you got apples?    ✓ Have you got apples?

3. Side-by-Side Comparison

Have/Has Got — sahiplik (possession)Do/Does — eylem/alışkanlık (action/habit)
Have you got cheese?Do you like cheese?
Has he got eggs?Does he eat eggs?
I haven't got bananas.I don't buy bananas.
She's got a shopping list.She makes a shopping list every week.

Ask yourself: Am I talking about owning/having something right now (have/has got) or about an action, habit, or opinion (do/does)?

Türkçe ipucu: Eğer cümlede "sahip olmak / var / yok" anlamı varsa → have/has got. Eğer bir alışkanlık, eylem ya da tercih anlatıyorsan → do/does.

4. Common Mistakes

✗ Incorrect✓ CorrectWhy
She have got a car.She has got a car.He/She/It needs "has", not "have".
Do she like tea?Does she like tea?He/She/It needs "does", not "do".
I don't have got money.I haven't got money.Never mix "don't" with "got".
Has you got a pen?Have you got a pen?"You" always takes "have", not "has".

📖 Reading 10 minutes

▾

Mehmet goes shopping every Saturday morning. He has got a shopping list on his phone, so he never forgets anything. This week, he has got milk, bread, eggs, and apples on his list. He doesn't buy cheese because he doesn't like the taste. His mum loves bananas, so he always buys six bananas for her.

At the supermarket, Mehmet puts everything in a trolley. He doesn't like long queues, so he usually goes early, before the shop gets busy. Today, there is a discount on orange juice, so he buys two bottles. At the checkout, the cashier gives him a receipt. Mehmet checks it carefully because he wants to make sure the prices are correct.

After shopping, Mehmet's mum asks him, "Have you got everything on the list?" Mehmet says, "Yes, I have — and I've also got a surprise for you!" He shows her the bananas, and she smiles.
