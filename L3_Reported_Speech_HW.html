Reading

A formal text using a wide range of reporting verbs — read and analyse

B2

At a recent industry panel on supply chain transparency, several senior figures shared their views on current challenges and future direction. The panel chair opened by acknowledging that disruption in global logistics had created serious difficulties for businesses of all sizes.

A logistics director insisted that her company had always operated with full transparency and denied having withheld information from its partners. She warned, however, that further regulatory pressure could force some smaller operators out of the market entirely.

A procurement specialist then proposed that companies adopt standardised reporting frameworks across the sector. He argued that the lack of common standards had made meaningful comparison between suppliers almost impossible. A second panellist urged the audience not to underestimate the role of technology in solving these issues, and promised that new tracking tools would be made available to the industry within the year.

When an audience member asked what governments could do to help, the chair recommended that policymakers engage directly with industry groups before drafting new legislation. She also urged businesses to document their processes more rigorously, suggesting that poor record-keeping had contributed to many of the current difficulties.

1. What did the logistics director insist and deny? What did she warn about?

2. What did the procurement specialist propose and argue?

3. What did the second panellist urge and promise?

4. Choose three reporting verbs from the text (other than "say") and explain what each one tells us about the speaker's tone, intention, or attitude.

5. The chair "recommended that policymakers engage directly..." — why is the base form (subjunctive) used here rather than "engages" or "engaged"? What pattern does "recommend" follow?
