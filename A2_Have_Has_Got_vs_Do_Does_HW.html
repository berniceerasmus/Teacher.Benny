📌 Quick Grammar Recap

▾

Have/Has got = possession → I've got bread. / Has she got milk?

Have (ordinary verb) = possession, but needs do/does → Do you have bread? / She doesn't have milk.

Do/Does = actions, habits, likes → Do you like apples? / He doesn't buy cheese.

Never mix: Does she have got...? is always wrong.

Türkçe: "Have got" sahiplik için kısa yoldur (do/does gerekmez). "Have" tek başına kullanıldığında do/does ister. "Do/does" ise alışkanlık ve eylemler içindir.
