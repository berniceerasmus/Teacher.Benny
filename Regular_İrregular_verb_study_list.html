Simple Present & Simple Past — Verb List

Simple Present & Simple Past · 100-verb list

How to study this list: Cover the "Simple Past" column with your hand or a paper. Look at the base verb, say the past form out loud, then check if you were right. Do this for every verb, every day, until you don't need to think about it. The green verbs are regular (just add -ed). The orange verbs are irregular — they change in their own special way, so they need extra memory work.
Spelling Rules

Simple Present — he / she / itGeniş zaman — o (he/she/it)

  • Most verbs: add -s → work → works Çoğu fiil: sonuna -s eklenir → work → works
  • Verbs ending in -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: add -es → watch → watches, go → goes -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o ile biten fiiller: sonuna -es eklenir → watch → watches, go → goes
  • Consonant + y: change y → i, add -es → study → studies Sessiz harf + y: y harfi i'ye döner, sonuna -es eklenir → study → studies
  • Vowel + y: just add -s → play → plays Sesli harf + y: sadece -s eklenir → play → plays

Simple Past — regular verbsGeçmiş zaman — düzenli fiiller

  • Most verbs: add -ed → work → worked Çoğu fiil: sonuna -ed eklenir → work → worked
  • Verbs ending in -e: just add -d → live → lived, like → liked -e ile biten fiiller: sadece -d eklenir → live → lived, like → liked
  • Consonant + y: change y → i, add -ed → study → studied Sessiz harf + y: y harfi i'ye döner, sonuna -ed eklenir → study → studied
  • Vowel + y: just add -ed → stay → stayed Sesli harf + y: sadece -ed eklenir → stay → stayed
  • One vowel + one consonant (short, stressed verb): double the last letter, then add -ed → plan → planned Tek sesli + tek sessiz harfle biten kısa fiiller: son harf tekrarlanır, sonra -ed eklenir → plan → planned

Irregular verbs — no rule!Düzensiz fiiller — kural yok!

Irregular verbs don't follow a spelling pattern. Each one changes in its own way — go → went, eat → ate, buy → bought. There's no shortcut here. The only way to learn them is repetition, out loud, every day, until they become automatic.

Düzensiz fiiller belirli bir yazım kuralına uymaz. Her biri kendine özgü bir şekilde değişir — go → went, eat → ate, buy → bought. Burada kısayol yoktur. Öğrenmenin tek yolu her gün sesli tekrar yaparak ezberlemektir.
