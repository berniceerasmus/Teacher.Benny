Singular & Plural Nouns

Tekil ve Çoğul İsimler — Ev Ödevi
A1–A2 60 MIN HOMEWORK
Quick reminder / Kısa hatırlatma:
  • Most words: add −s (book → books) — çoğu kelimeye -s eklenir
  • Words ending −s/−sh/−ch/−x/−o: add −es (box → boxes) — bu harflerle bitenlere -es
  • Consonant + y: change to −ies (city → cities) — ünsüz+y → ies
  • Some −f/−fe words: change to −ves (leaf → leaves) — f/fe → ves
  • Irregular words don't follow a rule (man → men, child → children) — düzensizler ezberlenir
Check your answers as you go — the button under each exercise tells you right away if you're correct. / Cevaplarınızı ilerledikçe kontrol edin.

H3 Reading passage

Okuma parçası

A Saturday at the Market

Every Saturday, my grandmother goes to the market with her two daughters. The market is busy and full of people. There are stalls with fresh potatoes, tomatoes, and bread. Near the entrance, two old men sell cheese and olives from small wooden boxes.

My grandmother always buys presents for her three grandchildren. Last week, she bought us new shoes and a bag of sweet oranges. My little cousins are still babies, so she also buys them soft toys.

After the market, we walk home together. On the way, we always stop to look at the fish in the small pond near the park, and count how many birds are sitting on the fence. It is my favourite part of the week.
