Part 1 · Grammar

01 What is singular / plural?

Tekil ve çoğul nedir?

In English, every noun (a word for a person, animal, place or thing) has two forms: singular, for one, and plural, for more than one. Getting this right matters because it changes the verb you use ("There is a book" vs "There are two books") and it's one of the very first things a listener notices about your English.

Singular = ONE person, animal, place or thing. Plural = MORE THAN ONE.

Tekil = BİR kişi, hayvan, yer veya şey. Çoğul = BİRDEN FAZLA.

a book (bir kitap) → two books (iki kitap) a student (bir öğrenci) → five students (beş öğrenci)
In use: "I have a book." (singular, use "a") → "I have three books." (plural, no "a", add −s)

02 Regular plurals — just add −s

Düzenli çoğullar — sona −s eklenir

Most English nouns are "regular" — they follow one simple pattern. This is the rule you will use most often, so it's worth making automatic.

Most nouns simply take −s at the end. The −s is usually pronounced like a soft /s/ or /z/ sound, not a new syllable.

Çoğu kelimenin sonuna sadece −s eklenir. Bu −s genellikle yumuşak bir /s/ veya /z/ sesi gibi okunur, yeni bir hece oluşturmaz.

book kitap→books car araba→cars table masa→tables pen kalem→pens
In use: "My sister has two cars and four pens in her bag."

03 Add −es

Sonu −s, −ss, −sh, −ch, −x, −o ile bitenler — −es eklenir

Some word endings make a plain −s hard to pronounce, so English adds an extra vowel sound: −es. This adds a new syllable you can hear.

Words ending in −s, −ss, −sh, −ch, −x, −o add −es.

Bu harflerle biten kelimelere, telaffuzu kolaylaştırmak için −es eklenir.

bus otobüs→buses class sınıf→classes brush fırça→brushes watch saat→watches box kutu→boxes potato patates→potatoes
Note: piano → pianos, photo → photos (some −o words just add −s). / Bazı −o ile biten kelimeler sadece −s alır.
In use: "There are five buses and two boxes outside the school."

04 Consonant + Y → −ies

Ünsüz + y → y, i olur ve −es eklenir

Words ending in "y" split into two groups, depending on the letter right before the y.

If the word ends in a consonant + y, change y → i and add −es. If it ends in a vowel + y, just add −s — the y stays the same.

Ünsüz harften sonra y varsa, y harfi i olur ve −es eklenir. Ünlü harften sonra y varsa, sadece −s eklenir, y değişmez.

baby bebek→babies city şehir→cities country ülke→countries boy erkek çocuk→boys day gün→days
In use: "We visited three cities in two days."

05 −f / −fe → −ves

Sonu −f / −fe ile bitenler — f, v olur

A small group of −f and −fe words soften the f sound to v in the plural. This group is small enough to memorise.

Some words ending in −f or −fe change to −ves.

Bazı −f veya −fe ile biten kelimelerde f harfi v olur ve −ves eklenir.

leaf yaprak→leaves knife bıçak→knives wife eş→wives life hayat→lives
Exceptions / İstisnalar: roof → roofs, chef → chefs, belief → beliefs.
In use: "She put the knives next to the leaves on the table." (an unusual sentence, but easy to remember!)

These words are some of the most common nouns in English, and none of them follow a spelling rule. Because you will hear and use them constantly, it's worth memorising this short list first, before the rarer regular words.

These common words don't follow any rule. They must be memorised.

Bu sık kullanılan kelimeler hiçbir kurala uymaz. Ezbere öğrenilmelidir.

07 Same singular & plural

Tekil ve çoğulu aynı olan kelimeler

A few words never change — the sentence (a number, or "many"/"some") tells you if it's one or many.

Bazı kelimeler hiç değişmez — tekil mi çoğul mu olduğunu cümledeki sayı veya "many/some" gibi kelimeler gösterir.

one sheep bir koyun→ten sheep one fish bir balık→three fish one deer bir geyik→many deer
In use: "I saw one fish." vs "I saw ten fish." (same word!)

08 Uncountable nouns — no plural

Sayılamayan isimler — çoğulu yoktur

Some nouns describe things we don't count one-by-one in English (even if we can in Turkish), like liquids, ideas, or general categories. These never take −s.

These nouns have no plural form. Use some / any instead of a number, or count them with a "piece/glass/cup of..." phrase.

Bu isimlerin çoğul hali yoktur. Sayı yerine some / any kullanılır; ya da "a piece of / a glass of" gibi ifadelerle sayılır.

water su money para information bilgi advice tavsiye furniture mobilya rice pirinç
I need some information. (NOT "an information" / "informations") — Biraz bilgiye ihtiyacım var.
In use: "Can you give me some advice?" / "I bought some furniture for my new flat."

P3 Reading passage

Okuma parçası

My Street

I live on a quiet street with my family. There are ten houses on our street, and each house has a small garden. In the mornings, I see two men walking their dogs and three women going to work. A yellow bus stops here every day — the children from our street get on the bus and go to school together.

Next to my house, there is a small shop. The shop sells fruit, vegetables, and fresh bread. My mother always buys some potatoes and two loaves of bread there. Behind the shop, there are three old trees with big green leaves. In autumn, the leaves fall onto the street.

On Saturday mornings, I clean my shoes and walk to the park with my two brothers. We watch the fish in the small lake and count the birds in the trees. It is a simple street, but I love it very much.
